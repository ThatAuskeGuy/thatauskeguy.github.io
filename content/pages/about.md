Hi! I'm Austin Hardesty, a husband, a dad, and a self-taught Python programmer, among many other things.

## Why did you start this blog?

I started this blog as a way to document my learning journey in programming, but also to have a place to write down the other things I have going on in life. Also, I wanted a place to show off the things I'm working on, which you can find out more about in the Projects tab.

## My Background

I didn't go to college for computer science. In fact, my educational background has nothing to do with computers or programming. I earned a Bachelor's degree in Professional FLight in 2018. I have a Multi-Commercial Certificate with Instrument Rating, and right after college, I started working as a professional pilot for a Part 91 operator. Unfortunately, a couple of months after, I was let go, and I didn't have enough hours to get another pilot job.

Soon after losing that job, I started exploring Linux SysAdmin, which eventually led into learning Python and Machine Learning. All of this was while I was working as a groundskeeper at the university I graduated from.

About a year and a half later, I joined my current employer as a Technical Support Specialist, but I also help with billing. I used my previous Python skills to help automate our billing and reduced the time to complete it from two weeks to about four days. I've been using Python to build internal tools at work since.

These days, I'm busy developing my programming skills by building real projects. You can find out more about these in my [Projects](/pages/projects.html).
